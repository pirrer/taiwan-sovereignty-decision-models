# (A) Verdict

**Major revision; not ready for arXiv in the present compiled form.** The v3 redesign is a substantial improvement: the full 1,215-job design was run three times for each model, request and raw-response records are retained, the E1 index is now appropriately qualified, the main numerical tables reproduce, the personas are localized, E3 uses matched sentences and separate judgments, and E5 uses opaque IDs. Two submission blockers remain: the PDF visibly contains an unfinished `_SENTENCE` placeholder, and the data/code URL is still `REPO_URL`. In addition, the paper's E5 conclusion is contradicted by the raw Jev choices, Haiku's partially missing repetitions are not adequately disclosed or handled by the advertised sensitivity analysis, and several causal/general claims remain stronger than the small purposive controls warrant. After correcting the blockers, repairing E5 and missingness reporting, and narrowing the E2–E4 and repeatability claims as listed below, the paper would be suitable for arXiv as a descriptive audit; no wholesale rerun is required for that narrower claim.
