# (A) Verdict

**Major revision; not ready for arXiv submission in its present form.** The principal tables are, for the most part, reproducible from the released JSONL files, and the large Traditional–Simplified differences for several models are real features of this dataset. However, the paper overstates what those differences establish. Its central exact-determinism claim is false for Jev and is based on a very narrow repeat experiment; five reported Haiku refusals are actually parser failures; “5,505 queries” counts job records rather than API requests; missing answers and varying denominators are not disclosed; the stance score combines heterogeneous factual, legal, normative, and predictive propositions; and E2–E4 do not isolate script, topic, persona, or policy effects as cleanly as the prose claims. The external-source audit is also incomplete: browser access was denied during this review, so I could corroborate the OpenAI, TypeSafe, and Cloudflare product facts only against the repository’s contemporaneous notes and cited official URLs, while several literature and benchmark claims remain unsupported by auditable source material in the submission.
